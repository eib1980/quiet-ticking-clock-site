Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Quiet Ticking Clock does not collect, store, transmit or share personal data.

Data collection

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Local settings

Your app preferences, such as the selected sound, volume, clock style and appearance settings, are stored locally on your device.

Third parties

Quiet Ticking Clock does not send personal information to third parties.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this policy will be updated accordingly.
